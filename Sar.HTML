    Product Description
    Flowy Net Fabric

    This saree has a very light and gently flowing design since it has been made from durable and soft net fabric. Its plush texture adds a touch of refinement, and even after multiple washings, it maintains its bright colour without wrinkling. Moreover, the semi-sheer texture of the net fabric enhances the overall charm of the saree.

    Meticulous Embroidery

    The intricate embroidery of flowers on this saree gives a touch of charm and elegance to this traditional Indian garment. Moreover, the embroidered patterns ensure that they won't wear off fast and rather remain intact for long, maintaining the saree's overall appeal.

    Unstitched Blouse Piece

    This saree incorporates an unstitched blouse piece, which increases its versatility. It enables you to have the blouse stitched to your specifications and preferences in regard to style. You can get it stitched in a number of styles and sleeve types to create customised looks. To achieve your desired style, you can alter the blouse's neckline and sleeve length.

    Versatile Wear

    This saree can be worn on a wide range of occasions, from festivals and family gatherings. You can pair it up with statement pieces of jewellery and footwear for formal events or keep it more casual with minimal accessories for daytime celebrations. The saree's ability to adapt to different settings and levels of formality makes it a valuable addition to your wardrobe.

    Minimal Maintenance

    There is hardly much maintenance required for this saree. In order to preserve its colour and texture, hand wash it in cool, running water with a mild detergent. Bleach and strong chemicals should be avoided since they can damage cloth. To avoid stretching, gently press out excess water without twisting the saree. The saree can be protected from harm or fading over time by being stored in a dry, cool place out of direct sunlight.
